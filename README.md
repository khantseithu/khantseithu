## What I've Shipped

### Products (DASOMX)

- 💬 **OKCHAT v2** - multi-tenant AI chatbot platform with hybrid RAG, MCP tools, Slack and Discord bots, and LiveKit voice agents
  - 1,061 organizations · 25K+ conversations a month · 35% lower LLM cost from routing and fallback across five providers
- 🎓 **LEIA** - AI learning platform: source-grounded courses, quizzes, and podcasts, AI tutors, real-time voice. Sole engineer
